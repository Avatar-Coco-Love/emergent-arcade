            About the arcade

            Emergent Arcade is a growing collection of tiny experiments in emergent game design.

            The rules every game follows

            • Two or three core verbs (tap, drag, hold…), each doing something different.
            • The verbs share state: each one reads a value another one writes, so they tangle.
            • One scene, simple shapes, no art or audio files. One self-contained HTML file.

            How the games are made

            An AI proposes and builds each game within those rules. Headless bots balance it, a human reviews it, and then your ratings and anonymous play stats decide whether it gets revised or retired. Retired games stay playable in the archive.
